Relay - get started with friends

Your account and servers
1. Open Relay. Sign in or choose Create account; no invitation is required.
2. The account form opens inside Relay when WebView2 is available.
   You can also choose to sign in with your browser.
3. Choose Create a server, or join with a friend's invitation.
4. Server encryption is off by default (recommended). You can turn it on when creating a server.

Invite a friend
1. Open Invite friends > Share and copy invitation.
2. Relay prepares the iroh connection and copies the invitation.
3. Send the invitation privately to your friend.
4. Keep Relay and the host PC running while friends use your server.
Advanced connection choices include Tailscale and direct addresses.
Copy invitation is also available for an already prepared connection.

Join friends
Open the invitation link in a browser and allow it to open the installed Relay.
You can also paste the invitation in Join friends and choose Join server.
If connection fails, ask the host to keep Relay open, then choose Try again.
A local network address alone cannot reach a friend in another home.
If a protected conversation requests confirmation, follow Settings > Encryption.
Keep device keys; an account password cannot restore lost private keys.

Closing and updating
Relay starts quietly with Windows after installation or an update.
Turn this off in Settings > Advanced > Start Relay with Windows.
The cross hides Relay in the Windows notification area by default.
Right-click its icon > Quit Relay to exit. Hosted servers finish closing.
Run a newer installer from https://github.com/TheBengine/Relay/releases.
Its Stop my local server and continue option requests normal server shutdown.
Do not uninstall first. Accounts, keys and history are preserved.

Data and removal
Local server data normally lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Relay\server-data.
Uninstall through Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Relay.
Uninstall removes the program and preserves your data.
